HOW TO PLAY

Move

Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left half. That's your stick.

Up jump · Down crouch · Flick forward twice dash

Hold away from your opponent to block. Crouch-block stops low sweeps. Stand-block stops jump attacks.

Attack

PUNCH is fast · KICK hits hard · Down + KICK sweeps

SPECIAL fires an energy blast. Fill your meter and SPECIAL becomes a SUPER rush.

THROW grabs a blocking opponent up close. Blocking can't stop it.

Combos

Land a hit and the next button cancels into it:

PUNCH › PUNCH › KICK › SPECIAL

↓PUNCH › ↓PUNCH › ↓KICK

Win 2 rounds to take the match.

Keyboard: arrows/WASD move · J punch · K kick · L special · I throw · P pause
